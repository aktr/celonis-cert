## process mining
- real-time digital twin of your business processes
- x-ray of how yout business is running
  -  precise, 
  -  real-time

## case centric process mining (CCPM)
- like a event log
- tracks one type of object per process
  - Example: process = order management, object = sales order

## object centric process mining (OCPM)
- OCPM models your processes by capturing objects, events, and their relationships instead of by manually building isolated event logs.
